Free to connect

  • Your own web presence: a free private-by-default artist, creator or instructor account and profile, with owner-controlled visibility.
  • Public artist showcase: publish your approved basic artist page across the connected Intentional Noise sites when you choose; visitors can browse published work without registering.
  • Browse and connect: explore public artist profiles and gig opportunities without signing up; free member sign-in unlocks private member search, current contact tools and gig-request participation. No booking commission from us.
  • 5 work previews — audio or video, 60 seconds each, to demonstrate your work in the public or owner-approved showcase.
  • 5 FROG release cards with your own links and artwork.
  • Private collaboration space: Connect Studio offers eligible members a private project, real recording uploads or external media links, and invited collaborators. New free Studio pilot accounts have a 100-place capacity, one project, five track records and two invited collaborators; founding workspaces keep their existing privileges.
  • Storage solutions: choose one preferred external storage provider or your own storage, save a private shortcut and share only the individual approved links you select. Providers such as pCloud, iCloud Drive, OneDrive and Google Drive bill their own storage plans.

When costs start

  • More than 5 previews or longer videos? Those exceed the free preview allowance. Link to media you host elsewhere; no paid Connect expansion is on sale yet.
  • More than 5 release cards? Replace or remove an existing card. No paid extra slots are offered yet.
  • Storage providers: Connect provides the choice and one private storage shortcut; it does not supply unlimited cloud storage or automatically synchronize provider files. Provider free-space limits and paid plans are managed by the provider you choose.
  • Extras: New merchant websites, additional saved storage-provider shortcuts, expanded Studio capacity, professional media and promotion may be separately priced if offered. Always optional.

What stays guaranteed free with the one-time Founder payment?

$12.00 once, for an approved account. After verification is approved by Intentional Noise and a successful one-time payment is recorded, the account's then-current included free features stay free if base membership prices change. This is a price guarantee, not a subscription, mandatory joining fee or automatic upgrade.

  • Web presence and basic artist showcase: your existing free profile and eligibility to publish an approved artist showcase under current rules and limits.
  • Private collaboration space: current Studio project and collaborator privileges where your account is eligible, subject to its existing pilot capacity and safety limits.
  • Storage solutions: the current free ability to choose, change and save one preferred external storage shortcut, plus approved media-link sharing. Provider capacity and provider fees are not included.
  • Your current free tools: member search, current gig-request access, resource guides, up to five 60-second work previews and five FROG release cards under existing limits.

Not included: new optional paid features, additional or unlimited provider storage, future merchant websites, third-party charges or new paid Studio upgrades. Free public browsing and basic registration remain free without the $12 payment.
