What's free? What's paid?

Finding people and opportunities is free. You only pay when you choose an outside service or a future optional upgrade.

Free to connect

  • One account and your artist or creator profile.
  • Browse talent, find gigs, message members and post event requests. No booking commission from us.
  • 5 work previews — audio or video, 60 seconds each.
  • 5 FROG release cards with your own links and artwork.
  • Publish your basic artist profile across the connected sites when you choose.

Open Intentional Noise Connect →

When costs start

  • More than 5 previews or longer videos? Those exceed the free preview allowance. Link to media you host elsewhere; no paid Connect expansion is on sale yet.
  • More than 5 release cards? Replace or remove an existing card. No paid extra slots are offered yet.
  • Storage: Your chosen storage provider may charge when its free space or bandwidth is used up. Large files, backups and delivery cost money. You choose and pay that provider directly.
  • Extras: Custom websites, professional media and promotion may be separately priced if offered. Always optional.

No paid Connect plan or checkout is active today. Nothing upgrades automatically. Any future fee must be shown and accepted before you're charged. Artists and event organizers agree on performance fees directly.
